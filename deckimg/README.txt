PMT flashcard images
====================

The images in this folder are pages of flashcard sets by PMT Education
(Physics & Maths Tutor, https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/), shown on
IAL Revise as picture flashcards.

This work by PMT Education is licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).

The pages are reproduced unchanged (rendered at a lower resolution for screens).
They are used for free, non-commercial study only. IAL Revise is not affiliated
with or endorsed by PMT Education. The original sets are linked from each quiz
on the site.

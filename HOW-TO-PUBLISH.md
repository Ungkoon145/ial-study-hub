# How to publish IAL Revise (free, about 10 minutes)

These steps put the site on GitHub Pages, which is free. Your address will look like
`https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ial-study-hub/`.

## 1. Unzip
Unzip `ial-study-hub-website.zip`. You get a folder with `index.html`, the `.js` files,
`manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and an `icons` folder. Upload **all** of them, keeping `icons` as a folder.

## 2. Make a GitHub account
Go to github.com and sign up (free). Verify your email.

## 3. Create a repository
1. Click **+** (top right), then **New repository**.
2. Repository name: `ial-study-hub` (this becomes part of your address).
3. Choose **Public**.
4. Click **Create repository**.

## 4. Upload the files
1. On the new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**.
2. Drag in everything from the unzipped folder, including the `icons` folder.
   (On a computer, Chrome or Edge handles folder drag-and-drop best.)
3. Scroll down and click **Commit changes**.

## 5. Turn on GitHub Pages
1. In the repository, open **Settings**, then **Pages** (left menu).
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Branch: **main**, folder: **/ (root)**. Click **Save**.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes, then refresh. The page shows your site’s address.

## 6. Share and install
- Send the address to friends.
- iPhone/iPad: open it in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen, like an app.
- Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app**.

## Updating the site later
Upload the changed files to the same repository (same names) and commit.
Visitors get the new version the next time they open the site online.

## Good to know
- Everyone’s progress is saved in their own browser on that device. It is private to them and is not sent anywhere.
  Settings has **Download backup** and restore, for moving to a new phone or browser.
- The app works offline once opened. Past papers, mark schemes, examiner reports and notes open from
  Physics & Maths Tutor and Pearson, so those need internet.
- The site only links to those resources; it does not copy them.

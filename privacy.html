Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

IAL Revise is a free, independent revision site for Pearson Edexcel International A Level students. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson or Physics & Maths Tutor. Pearson and Edexcel are trademarks of Pearson Education Ltd. Credits and licences for the material it shows are listed in Settings, under Credits and licences. This page explains what we store and why, in plain words.

Using the site without an account

Everything you do (your subjects, marks, flashcards, mistakes, notes and profile) is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We cannot see it.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google, with Microsoft, with an email and password, or with a sign-in link sent to your email. We store the following in our database (Google Firebase) so your progress can follow you between devices:

  • your email address, your name and a user ID
  • your study progress and your profile (name, school, year, subjects, photo if you add one)

Passwords are handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores them in a scrambled (hashed) form. IAL Revise never sees or stores your password. Emails for verifying your address, resetting your password or signing in come from Firebase on our behalf, and are only sent when you ask for them.

We use this only to run the site for you. We do not sell it, show adverts, or share it with anyone. Only you can read your progress: our database rules block everyone else.

Weekly summary for a parent

This is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the site writes a short summary of your week (study time, numbers of reviews, papers and mistakes, exams coming up, and, only if you tick them, your average score and predicted grades). You see it first. It is only sent if you choose to send it yourself, by email, LINE, your phone’s share menu or copy and paste. The site never sends it for you, and it never contains your mistakes, notes or answers.

Friends

Friends is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your profile is encrypted separately for each friend you add, so only those friends can read it. Other users, and we as the site owner, cannot. Your marks, mistakes and notes are never shared. Turning Friends off removes your profile from the database.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything stored with it. Signing out removes your progress from that browser but keeps it in your account.

Under 18?

Many of our users are school students. If you are under the age where your country's law needs a parent's consent to use online services, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in. You can always use the site without an account.

Links to other sites

Past papers, mark schemes and notes open on Pearson's and Physics & Maths Tutor's own websites, which have their own privacy policies.
